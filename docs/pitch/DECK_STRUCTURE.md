## Live-demo script beats

1. Dashboard: Lethabo has a clear next action, not a generic content library.
2. Targeted activity: the recommendation leads directly to a real question.
3. Misconception moment: `(x - 5)^2`, very sure, produces a learner-friendly explanation.
4. Retry: `(x - 5)(x + 5)` is correct; the previous response remains evidence.
5. Progress: correct retry does not automatically become strong understanding.

## Slide design guardrails

- Keep slides 1–4 mostly text/diagram-led, then show the product live.
- Use one or two screenshots only as a resilient anchor and fallback; do not turn the deck into a product tour.
- Separate current evidence from roadmap or proposed funding channels with explicit labels.
