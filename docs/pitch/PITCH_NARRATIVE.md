# Project Odysseus pitch narrative

## Pitch thesis

**Marks tell us what happened. Project Odysseus helps tutors understand what evidence sits behind a learner's response, what to work on next, and whether understanding improves over time. Technology helps the tutor see more clearly; the tutor still teaches.**

## 1. Opening

A learner can receive 62% for Algebra. That tells us performance was weak in that assessment. It does not always tell a tutor what mathematical pattern caused the difficulty, what to reteach first, or whether one corrected answer reflects reliable understanding.

Project Odysseus combines CAPS-aligned human tutoring with learning intelligence designed to make the next intervention clearer.

## 2. The problem

The intervention gap is practical, not abstract:

```text
Learner completes work
        ↓
Gets a mark
        ↓
Tutor knows something went wrong
        ↓
Must manually interpret what to reteach next
```

Generic revision can consume scarce tutoring time without addressing the learner's actual method. A mark is useful context, but it is not a complete explanation of how the learner approached the work.

## 3. The insight

Better tutoring decisions need better evidence about the learner's approach, not only their final score. Project Odysseus can retain response correctness and patterns, confidence, repeated attempts, misconception evidence, and progress across time.

These are signals, not labels. No single response, confidence selection, or correct retry proves mastery. The purpose is to help a tutor ask a better next question and choose a more focused intervention.

## 4. The solution

Project Odysseus is CAPS-aligned tutoring supported by a platform that turns learner work into structured evidence and targeted next steps.

```text
Learner response
        ↓
Evaluation
        ↓
Learning evidence
        ↓
Targeted intervention
        ↓
Practice
        ↓
Progress over time
```

The human relationship remains central: tutors teach, explain, judge context, and decide how to act. The platform makes the evidence behind that work more visible and consistent.

## 5. The Golden Demo story

Show a Grade 9 Mathematics learner, Lethabo Mokoena, whose next action is Difference of Two Squares practice. Ask the audience to watch the second question:

```text
Factorise: x² − 25

Learner answer: (x − 5)²
Confidence: Very sure
```

Odysseus does not stop at "incorrect". It identifies evidence consistent with confusing a difference of two squares with the square of a difference, then gives the learner a contrast:

```text
a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b)
```

The learner retries with `(x − 5)(x + 5)` and receives a correct result. Make two distinctions explicit:

1. The original incorrect answer remains learning evidence; the retry does not erase it.
2. A correct retry is not automatically called mastery; progress is governed by accumulated, versioned evidence.

## 6. What to avoid saying

Do not call Project Odysseus an AI tutor, an LMS, a worksheet website, a chatbot, a replacement for teachers, or a mark tracker. Do not claim that it diagnoses a learner definitively or guarantees improvement. The pitch is about evidence-informed human tutoring.
