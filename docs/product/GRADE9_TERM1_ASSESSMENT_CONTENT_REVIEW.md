Each independent Whole Numbers form therefore has 18 questions and 30 minutes: four distinct primary-skill questions for every critical skill. ## Curriculum coverage

- **Whole Numbers:** real-number classification; factors, prime factorisation, HCF/LCM; ratio/rate; direct and inverse proportion; commission, rental and compound-interest applications.
- **Integers:** signed operations, brackets and order, squares/cubes and roots, absolute values, temperature context, and sign-error diagnosis.
- **Exponents:** product, quotient and power laws, zero and negative exponents, simplification, equivalence and domain reasoning. Scientific notation is not required for mastery.
- **Numeric and Geometric Patterns:** first differences, tables, nth-term rules, joined-square matchsticks using M(n) = 3n + 1, verification and generalisation-error diagnosis.

## Independence and mathematical review

Each endline, mastery challenge and reassessment uses its own question codes and prompt/solution/answer data. Automated checks found no duplicate question codes or exact prompt collisions inside a form. Parameterised numerical families are intentionally listed in the export and remain a reviewer focus: automated structure cannot certify that two examples are pedagogically independent or that a conceptual explanation is best-in-class.
